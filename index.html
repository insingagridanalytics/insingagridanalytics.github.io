Electricity access

Geospatial electrification planning

Least-cost electrification plans for universal access. For every settlement, we show whether grid extension, a mini-grid or solar home systems is the cheapest way to provide electricity, and the best routes for extending the grid.

How it works
  1. Collect data from public and private energy sector players and integrate it into one GIS.
  2. Map where people live and estimate the electricity needs of households, schools, health centres and businesses.
  3. Map the existing network and where it has capacity for new connections.

Energy losses

Energy loss reduction

We compare energy sent out with energy billed for each feeder and transformer area, then separate technical losses (energy lost as heat in lines and transformers) from non-technical losses (metering faults, billing errors and illegal connections). We also check whether load is spread evenly across the three phases of each transformer, since uneven phases overheat equipment and add to losses.

What you get

  • Loss heat maps with technical and non-technical losses shown separately
  • Inspection priority lists for field teams
  • Phase imbalance by transformer and lists of customers to reconnect
Data sharing

Governed data sharing and data governance

Researchers, funders, regulators and vendors regularly ask utilities for data. We build platforms where the utility decides what is released, partners receive approved samples only, and partner code runs on the full data inside the utility. We also set up data ownership, update procedures and sharing rules aligned with national data protection law.

Used for research partnerships, funder and regulator reporting, vendor evaluation and off-grid coordination.
How it works
  1. Data owners approve which fields can be shared, and how much.
  2. Sampling happens inside the utility's own extraction process.
  3. Access is granted per project and role, with sensitive fields masked and an expiry date.
  4. Partners submit code; the utility reviews it and runs it on the full data. Only results leave.
  5. Every release, access and refusal is recorded in a log that cannot be edited.
