The problems we solve

Does this sound like your organisation?

Energy losses

You know energy is being lost, but not where.

Between the transformer and the customer's meter, utilities struggle to measure how much energy goes missing, or to separate technical losses (energy lost as heat in lines and equipment) from non-technical losses (meter faults, billing errors, illegal connections). The result is lost revenue every month, and inspection teams sent to the wrong places.

How we help: Insinga LossFinder checks your network data, measures losses at every transformer from the data you already hold, and gives your crews a ranked list of meters to inspect.

For utilities and regulatorsSee LossFinder
Electricity access

You have connection targets, but no reliable plan to reach them.

Ministries and electrification programmes need to know who is still unserved, how far they are from the grid, and whether grid extension, a mini-grid or solar home systems is the cheapest way to reach them.

How we help: Insinga ConnectPlan maps who is unserved and shows the least-cost way to connect each area, by grid extension, mini-grid or solar home system.

For ministries, funders and mini-grid developersSee ConnectPlan
Untapped data

You hold years of data, but get little value from it.

GIS layers, billing records, meter readings and field surveys sit in separate systems that don't link to each other. Staff spend weeks preparing data before any analysis can start.

How we help: Insinga DataHub cleans, connects and catalogues your data into one reliable foundation, and Ask Insinga turns it into answers your teams can act on.

For utilities and energy agenciesSee DataHub
Data sharing

Researchers and partners need your data, but you can't share it safely.

Utilities rarely have a safe, structured way to share data, so requests stall or raw files are sent without control. Researchers, in turn, have no way to test their methods on real utility data.

How we help: with Insinga DataHub, the utility decides what to share, partners receive approved samples only, and every request is logged.

AI-assisted loss detection for low-voltage networks

Insinga LossFinder

Find where energy is lost between the transformer and the meter, without installing new meters. LossFinder compares the energy delivered to each transformer with the prepaid tokens and bills of the customers connected to it, separates technical losses (energy lost as heat in lines and transformers) from non-technical losses (bypassed, tampered or faulty meters), and ranks where to act.

Book a LossFinder demo
How it works
  1. Data health check. Finds customers linked to the wrong transformer, missing links and duplicate meters, which make loss figures wrong.
  2. Virtual transformer meters. An energy balance for every transformer, built from GIS, token sales and feeder or transformer readings.
  3. Inspection and revenue recovery. A ranked list of meters to inspect, with reasons. Engineers approve each visit and track what is recovered.

FAQ

Common questions

Do we need new meters or sensors?

No. Insinga starts from data you already hold: network GIS, billing, prepaid token sales, meter readings and feeder or transformer readings.

Will our data be used to train models for other utilities?

No. Your data is never used to train shared models. Each utility's results are built from its own data, for its own network.

Who can see our data?

Only the people you approve. Access is set by role, and every action and data release is logged.

Where does Insinga run?

On your own servers, in a local or national cloud, or hosted by us, depending on your data protection and data residency rules.

Does Insinga make decisions on its own?

No. It recommends corrections, plans and inspections. Your engineers and managers review and approve every action.

Can we start with only one product?

Yes. Each product is licensed separately. Most utilities start with a Network Data Health Check and LossFinder on one feeder, then add more.

What do you need from us to start a pilot?

The network map for the pilot area, a year of prepaid sales or billing data, and feeder or transformer readings if you have them. We agree data handling rules with you before anything is shared.
